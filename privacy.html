Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Who we are

Deskle is a small, independently-run daily word game. This policy explains what information is (and isn't) collected when you play.

Information we collect

Deskle does not require an account, sign-up, or any personal information to play. We do not collect your name, email address, or any way to identify you individually.

Your game stats — win streak, games played, and guess history — are stored only in your own browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data will erase it.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand overall traffic (page views, general visitor counts). This is a cookieless, privacy-first analytics service — it does not track you individually across sites and does not use cookies to identify you.

Advertising

Deskle may display advertising served by Google AdSense. If and when advertising is active, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and how to opt out of personalized advertising, at Google's Ads Policy page.

Children's privacy

Deskle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of Deskle after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via the contact page.
