About Deskle

Deskle is a small daily word-guessing game built for anyone who wants a genuinely short mental break — not a 20-minute scroll, just one word, guessed in a few tries, and back to work.

Why it exists

Office breaks are usually either too short to do anything, or so easy to fall into that they eat half an hour. Deskle is built around a single constraint: it should take about five minutes, start to finish, whether you play the daily puzzle or a few rounds of practice mode.

How it works

Pick a word length — 3, 4, or 5 letters — and guess the word of the day in six tries. Everyone gets the same word each day, so if you and a coworker both play, you're solving the exact same puzzle. Prefer to just keep playing? Practice mode serves up a fresh random word every round, no daily limit.

Who's behind it

Deskle is built and maintained independently, not by a large studio or company. It started as a simple idea — a genuinely quick word game for a work break — and has been refined based on real player feedback since.
